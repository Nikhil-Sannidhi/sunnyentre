Sannidhi (सन्निधि) — Sanskrit for “presence” or “nearness.” We bring expertise close to the causes that need it.

Our mission

Great ideas shouldn’t stall for lack of a consulting budget.

Mission-driven organizations often face the same challenges as any business — planning, fundraising, systems, measuring results — but rarely have the budget for professional advice.

We close that gap. Sannidhi Entreprizes is a pro bono consulting firm: we match vetted volunteer professionals to well-defined projects, manage the engagement, and make sure the work leaves your team stronger and more independent.

Presence

We show up, listen first, and work alongside your team — not around it.

Practicality

Recommendations sized to your resources, with tools you can run yourself.

Integrity

Confidential, conflict-free, and fully free of charge — no upsells.

How it works

From first call to lasting impact

  1. 01

    Tell us your need

    Share your organization and the challenge you’re facing through our short request form.

  2. 02

    Scope together

    We hold a discovery call to define clear goals, a timeline and the deliverables you’ll receive.

  3. 03

    Get matched

    We pair you with volunteer consultants whose skills and experience fit your project.

  4. 04

    Deliver & hand off

    We work side by side, then hand over the plan, tools and training so your team owns the results.
